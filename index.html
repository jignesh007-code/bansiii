05.01

THE THINGS THAT STAYED

Not accusations.
Just the things I wish I had known how to explain.

What hurt wasn't that you didn't give me attention every second.

It was the moments when I felt like the closeness between us disappeared when other people were around.

Sometimes I didn't need attention. I just wanted to feel that what existed between us was still there.

— WHAT I NEEDED

To feel that our connection didn't suddenly become invisible around other people.

It wasn't about minor disagreements or things going wrong. It was how quickly a small misunderstanding turned into coldness or defensiveness before we even talked about what happened.

Sometimes a small mistake felt like it carried the weight of the entire relationship.

— WHAT I NEEDED

Correct me without humiliating me. To know that a disagreement didn't mean you stopped caring.

There were days when I was struggling and overwhelmed, trying my best just to keep going. Instead of a soft place to land, I felt like I was being judged for not being at my best.

I wasn't asking for solutions, just patience while I was exhausted.

— WHAT I NEEDED

Listen before defending. A gentle moment of understanding when I was already holding too much.

Taking space is completely okay, but the silence without knowing why felt heavy. When withdrawal became the default response instead of talking things through, it left me sitting with unanswered questions.

It made the quiet feel louder than any argument.

— WHAT I NEEDED

Tell me when you need space, instead of leaving me wondering where I stand or what went wrong.

I didn't do things for praise or recognition. But when the care, late nights, and small gestures I put in felt unnoticed or dismissed, it made me question if anything I did truly mattered to you.

Effort hurts when it feels like it's only moving in one direction.

— WHAT I NEEDED

Don't make me guess where I stand. A simple acknowledgment that you saw the love I was trying to give.

The hardest moment was when it felt like we weren't a team anymore—like every issue was me versus you rather than both of us facing the problem together.

That was when the fear of losing what we built started feeling real.

— WHAT I NEEDED

Let us fix things together. To know that when things get difficult, we choose to stand on the same side.
